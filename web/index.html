    How it was made

    The page runs the plain-text edit list and architecture directly: it starts every parameter at zero and applies the edits in the browser. Your drawing never leaves this page.

    Drawing pipeline: strokes are redrawn with a pen 12% of the character's height, the bounding box is fitted into 20 × 20, and the centre of mass is moved to the centre, as MNIST was made.

    Percentages: a softmax of the class scores with temperature 0.5, typed by hand and never fitted. The "shown vs right" figures are measured on training images never looked at while writing the weights (MNIST train 6,000–9,000; EMNIST Letters train 60,000–65,200).
